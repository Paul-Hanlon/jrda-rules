# Introduction

**JRDA Rules** is an interactive learning web app for Junior Roller Derby Association (JRDA) rules. It helps skaters, coaches, and officials learn and test their knowledge of the ruleset through browsable rules, quizzes, a glossary, and real-world casebook scenarios.

## What it is

The app presents the JRDA ruleset in a structured, searchable format with age-appropriate reading levels, making it accessible for junior skaters of all ages.

## Who it's for

- Junior skaters wanting to understand the rules
- Coaches and officials looking for a reference and teaching tool
- Anyone preparing for JRDA certification or assessments

## Live app

The app is hosted on Firebase and built with Angular v21.
